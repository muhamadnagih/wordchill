How to Play WordChill

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries (English or Arabic!). After each guess, the tile colors reveal how close you are:

W Teal Green: Letter is in the word and in the correct spot.
O Coral Orange: Letter is in the word, but in another spot.
R Muted Slate: Letter is not in the word at all.
